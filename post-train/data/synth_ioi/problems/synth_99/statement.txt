EMERGENCY BEACON NETWORK

A mountain rescue network connects N stations with N - 1 bidirectional trails. Trail i connects U_i and V_i with length W_i. Initially every station u has demand weight A_u = 0.

Process Q events of two types:
- 1 u delta : change A_u by adding delta (note that A_u may become negative).
- 2 u       : compute sum_{v=1}^N A_v * dist(u, v), where dist(u, v) is the shortest trail distance between u and v.

Input
The first line contains N and Q.
Each of the next N - 1 lines contains U_i, V_i, and W_i.
Each of the next Q lines describes an event in one of the two formats above.

Output
For each type-2 event print the weighted distance sum on its own line.

Constraints
1 <= N <= 100000
1 <= Q <= 120000
1 <= W_i <= 1000
-1000 <= delta <= 1000

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
5 6
1 2 3
2 3 4
2 4 2
4 5 5
1 1 2
1 5 3
2 2
1 3 -1
2 4
2 1

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
27
19
23

Sample input 2 (file sample_2.in)
3 4
1 2 10
2 3 20
1 1 5
2 3
1 3 -2
2 1

Sample output 2 (file sample_2.out)
150
-60

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
