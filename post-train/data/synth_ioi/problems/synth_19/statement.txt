MERCENARY WATCH

A fortress wall must be guarded continuously across N discrete hours numbered 1 to N. M mercenaries apply for work: mercenary i offers to guard every integer hour from L_i to R_i inclusive in exchange for wage W_i.

Select a subset of mercenaries so that every hour 1, 2, ..., N is guarded by at least one hired mercenary, minimizing the total wage paid (or report -1 if it is impossible to cover all N hours).

Input
The first line contains N and M.
Each of the next M lines contains L_i, R_i, and W_i.

Output
Print a single integer: the minimum total wage, or -1 if impossible.

Constraints
1 <= N, M <= 200000
1 <= L_i <= R_i <= N
1 <= W_i <= 10^9

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
6 4
1 3 10
2 5 15
4 6 12
1 6 30

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
22

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
