RIVER COURIERS

Along a river are N docks numbered 1 to N from upstream to downstream. A message starts at dock 1 (with initial cost 0) and must reach dock N by a sequence of boat hops that always move strictly downstream (from some dock j to a dock i > j).

Each dock i has three parameters: fuel rate A_i, current factor D_i, and harbour fee F_i. Sending a boat directly from dock j to dock i (j < i) costs A_j * D_i + F_i.

For every dock i = 2, 3, ..., N, find the minimum total cost for the message to reach dock i starting from dock 1.

Input
The first line contains N.
The second line contains A_1 ... A_N.
The third line contains D_1 ... D_N.
The fourth line contains F_1 ... F_N.

Output
Print N - 1 lines: on the k-th line print the minimum total cost to reach dock k + 1.

Constraints
2 <= N <= 200000
1 <= A_i, D_i <= 10^6
0 <= F_i <= 10^11

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
4
5 2 8 1
3 6 4 10
10 5 7 2

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
35
27
52

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
