CARAVAN CONTRACTS

Over a trading season of N days (numbered 1 to N), a merchant guild has signed M caravan contracts. Contract i starts on day L_i, ends on day R_i, and yields profit W_i.

You are asked Q audit queries. Query j gives an interval [A, B]: compute the total profit of all contracts i that are COMPLETELY CONTAINED within [A, B] (that is, A <= L_i <= R_i <= B).

Input
The first line contains N, M, and Q.
Each of the next M lines contains L_i, R_i, and W_i.
Each of the next Q lines contains A and B.

Output
For each audit query print the total contained profit on its own line.

Constraints
1 <= N, M, Q <= 200000
1 <= L_i <= R_i <= N
1 <= W_i <= 10^6
1 <= A <= B <= N

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
6 5 3
1 3 10
2 4 20
2 3 5
4 6 15
1 6 100
1 4
2 6
1 6

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
35
40
150

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
