CITADEL ENCLOSURE

A central citadel stands at the origin (0, 0). Around it stand N watchtowers; watchtower i is at integer coordinates (X_i, Y_i). No watchtower stands at (0, 0), and no line through (0, 0) passes through two or more watchtowers.

Count how many unordered triples of distinct watchtowers {i, j, k} form a triangle that strictly encloses the origin (0, 0).

Input
The first line contains N.
Each of the next N lines contains X_i and Y_i.

Output
Print a single integer: the number of enclosing triangles.

Constraints
3 <= N <= 180000
-10^9 <= X_i, Y_i <= 10^9
No line through (0, 0) contains two or more watchtowers.

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
4
1 1
-2 1
0 -3
2 1

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
2

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
