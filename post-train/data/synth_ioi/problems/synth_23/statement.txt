CRYSTAL RESONANCE

A gallery displays N energy crystals in a row numbered 1 to N, where crystal i has charge A_i. You are asked Q experiments. Each experiment specifies L and R: choose ANY subset of crystals from positions L, L+1, ..., R (possibly the empty subset, whose XOR is 0) to maximize the bitwise XOR of their charges.

Input
The first line contains N and Q.
The second line contains A_1 ... A_N.
Each of the next Q lines contains L and R.

Output
For each experiment print the maximum achievable subset XOR on its own line.

Constraints
1 <= N, Q <= 200000
0 <= A_i < 2^30
1 <= L <= R <= N

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
5 4
3 5 10 2 8
1 2
2 4
1 5
4 4

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
6
15
15
2

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
