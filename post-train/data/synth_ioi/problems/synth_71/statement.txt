SKYLINE SHADOWS

On a flat plain, N rectangular solar canopies cast shadows on the ground. Canopy i covers the axis-aligned rectangle [X_{1,i}, X_{2,i}] x [Y_{1,i}, Y_{2,i}] (with X_{1,i} < X_{2,i} and Y_{1,i} < Y_{2,i}). Canopies may overlap arbitrarily.

Compute the exact total area of the union of all N rectangles.

Input
The first line contains N.
Each of the next N lines contains four integers X_{1,i}, Y_{1,i}, X_{2,i}, and Y_{2,i}.

Output
Print a single integer: the exact area of the union of the N rectangles.

Constraints
1 <= N <= 150000
0 <= X_{1,i} < X_{2,i} <= 10^9
0 <= Y_{1,i} < Y_{2,i} <= 10^9

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
3
1 1 5 4
3 2 7 6
6 1 8 3

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
27

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
