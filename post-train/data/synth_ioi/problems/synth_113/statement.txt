ORDINAL TRAIL CENSUS

A forest reserve has N clearings numbered 1 to N connected by N - 1 bidirectional trails of positive lengths forming a tree.

Consider the N * (N - 1) / 2 unordered pairs of distinct clearings {u, v} (1 <= u < v <= N) and sort their shortest-path distances in non-decreasing order: d_1 <= d_2 <= ... <= d_{N*(N-1)/2}.

Given K, find d_K (the K-th smallest distance) and the sum (d_1 + d_2 + ... + d_K) modulo 998244353.

Input
The first line contains N and K.
Each of the next N - 1 lines contains u, v, and w describing a trail of length w.

Output
Print two space-separated integers: d_K and (d_1 + ... + d_K) mod 998244353.

Constraints
2 <= N <= 75000
1 <= K <= N * (N - 1) / 2
1 <= w <= 10^8

Sample input 1 (file sample_1.in)
4 4
1 2 3
2 3 4
2 4 5

Sample output 1 (file sample_1.out)
7 19

Sample input 2 (file sample_2.in)
5 7
1 2 2
1 3 2
1 4 2
1 5 2

Sample output 2 (file sample_2.out)
4 20

Limits: 2 seconds and 1024 MiB of memory per test.
